# Form Reset — Week 1

A 7-day habit week, not a heavy training week. Every day has something to do
so the streak stays intact, but only 4 of the 7 days involve any loaded
lifting at all — 2 are pure active recovery + technique work (no weight,
just pattern practice), and 1 is cardio/mobility. Every lift is deliberately
downgraded from the 4-day hypertrophy program to a version that's easier to
keep tight form on — the goal this week is grooving the pattern, not moving
weight. Once form feels solid and controlled for a few sessions, that's the
signal to move back toward the hypertrophy program, not a fixed week count.
