# Guidelines

## Why this week is lighter
This isn't a deload from the hypertrophy program — it's a reset. Every
loaded exercise this week is a technique-friendlier version of what's in
`programs/hypertrophy/`, at a weight low enough that hitting every rep with
clean, controlled form should feel very achievable, not like a grind. If a
prescribed weight still feels shaky, drop it further — there's no benefit
to fighting a weight this week.

## Warm-Up (every training day, ~6-8 min)
Skip static stretching entirely — with hypermobility, it works against
joint stability rather than for it.
- 4-5 min light cardio — bike, brisk walk, or jumping jacks.
- Upper days: arm circles x10/direction, band pull-aparts x15, wrist
  circles x10/direction each hand.
- Lower days: leg swings x10/side, bodyweight squats x10, hip circles
  x10/side, ankle rocks x10/side.
- No ramp-up sets needed this week — every working weight is already the
  ramp-up weight.

## Rules for Every Training Day
- No locked-out joints at the top of any lift — soft elbow on presses,
  soft knee on squats/split squats. Non-negotiable.
- Effort target: every rep should feel clearly controlled, RIR 4-5 (stop
  well short of failure) — this week is about the movement, not the
  challenge.
- Control the lowering phase (2-3 sec) on every lift.
- Rest 60-90 sec between sets — there's no rush, use the extra time to
  reset position rather than cutting rest.
- If anything gives a sharp or "wobbly/unstable" feeling (different from
  normal muscle fatigue) — stop that exercise for the day.

## Active Recovery + Form Reinforcement Days
No loaded lifting. The point is pattern practice and blood flow, not a
workout. Film yourself on the squat and hinge drills from the side at least
once this week — you don't need to send it anywhere, just watch it back and
notice where the lean or wobble comes from.

## When to move on from this week
This is written as one week on purpose — check in after day 7 rather than
assuming a fixed number of weeks. Move back toward the 4-day hypertrophy
program (`programs/hypertrophy/`) once the lifts here feel consistently
controlled, not shaky, for a couple of sessions in a row. If it's not there
yet, repeat this week's structure with the same or slightly bumped weights
rather than jumping back to hypertrophy's loads.
