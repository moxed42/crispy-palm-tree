## Monday

### Tofu & Chickpea Bowl (Lunch)
**Prep time:** 25 min | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients**
- 200g extra-firm tofu, pressed
- 15mL soy sauce
- 15mL cornstarch
- 1 can (425g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 5mL olive oil
- Pinch salt, paprika
- 200g roasted sweet potato (pre-batch cooked)
- 250mL roasted veggies (broccoli/peppers)
- 15-22mL tahini
- Juice of 1/2 lemon (15mL)
- 15-30mL water
- Pinch salt (for dressing)

**Nutrition Facts**
- Calories: 540
- Protein: 32g
- Fat: 14g
- Carbs: 70g
- Fiber: 14g

**Instructions**
1. Pat tofu dry with paper towels. Press between two plates for 5-10 min to remove moisture.
2. Cube the pressed tofu into 2.5cm pieces.
3. Toss tofu cubes in soy sauce, then coat evenly with cornstarch.
4. Pat chickpeas dry with paper towels. Toss with 5mL olive oil, salt, and paprika.
5. Air fry tofu at 200°C for 12 min, shaking the basket halfway through.
6. Air fry chickpeas at 200°C for 10-12 min, shaking halfway through (use a separate batch if space is limited).
7. While cooking, whisk tahini, lemon juice, 15-30mL water, and a pinch of salt until smooth. Add more water if the dressing is too thick.
8. Warm sweet potato and roasted veggies (microwave 1-2 min, or air fry 3-4 min at 200°C).
9. Assemble: sweet potato and roasted veggies as the base, top with tofu and chickpeas, drizzle with tahini sauce.

### Tofu Steaks (Dinner)
**Prep time:** 20 min | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients**
- 200g extra-firm tofu, pressed
- 15mL soy sauce
- 5mL ginger-garlic paste
- 500mL broccoli and carrots, chopped (approx 300g)
- 5mL olive oil
- Salt, pepper to taste
- 160mL cooked farro (dry weight ~55g)

**Nutrition Facts**
- Calories: 480
- Protein: 30g
- Fat: 12g
- Carbs: 58g
- Fiber: 9g

**Instructions**
1. Press tofu for 5-10 min as above.
2. Slice pressed tofu into 3-4 slabs (approximately 1.3cm thick each).
3. Combine soy sauce and ginger-garlic paste in a shallow bowl. Marinate tofu slices for at least 10 min.
4. Air fry tofu at 200°C for 15 min, flipping halfway through.
5. Toss broccoli and carrots with 5mL olive oil, salt, and pepper.
6. Air fry veggies at 200°C for 12-15 min (can run before or after tofu, or simultaneously in a second basket).
7. Cook farro ahead: per package directions, or pressure cooker method: 85mL farro + 170mL water, high pressure 10 min, quick release.
8. Plate tofu steaks and roasted veggies over farro.

## Tuesday

### Ground Turkey Taco Bowl (Lunch)
**Prep time:** 15 min | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients**
- 150g ground turkey
- 2.5mL chili powder
- 2.5mL paprika
- 2.5mL dried oregano
- Pinch salt
- 160mL cooked rice (dry weight ~55g)
- 125mL black beans, canned and drained
- 1 small handful shredded lettuce (approx 30g)
- 30mL salsa
- 1/4 medium avocado, sliced (approx 50g)

**Nutrition Facts**
- Calories: 550
- Protein: 34g
- Fat: 16g
- Carbs: 60g
- Fiber: 8g

**Instructions**
1. Heat a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat (no oil needed).
2. Add ground turkey, breaking it up with a spatula as it cooks.
3. Season with chili powder, paprika, oregano, and salt. Cook for 8-10 min until browned through (internal temp 74°C).
4. Warm black beans separately in microwave (2-3 min) or small pot.
5. Build the bowl: rice on the bottom, then turkey and beans on top, then lettuce, salsa, and avocado slices.

### Ground Beef & Veggie Stir-Fry (Dinner)
**Prep time:** 15 min | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients**
- 150g Walden Local 80/20 ground beef
- 500mL mixed stir-fry veggies (fresh or frozen, approx 300g)
- 15mL soy sauce
- 5mL ginger-garlic paste
- 2.5mL sesame oil (optional)
- 160mL cooked rice (dry weight ~55g)

**Nutrition Facts** (with fat drained, not rinsed)
- Calories: 560
- Protein: 26g
- Fat: 28g
- Carbs: 50g
- Fiber: 4g

**Instructions**
1. Heat a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add ground beef, breaking it apart with a spatula as it cooks, about 6-8 min.
3. Tilt the pan and spoon off rendered fat (drain into a small bowl or directly into trash). Alternatively, transfer cooked beef to a strainer and rinse briefly with hot water for a leaner result.
4. Return beef to the pan. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Add stir-fry veggies and soy sauce. Stir-fry for 4-5 min until crisp-tender.
6. Drizzle with sesame oil if using.
7. Serve over rice.

### Chana Masala (Dinner)
**Prep time:** 20 min | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients**
- 1 can (425g) chickpeas, drained (or 350mL cooked chickpeas)
- 1/2 medium onion, diced (approx 60g)
- 5-10mL ginger-garlic paste
- 1 can (400mL) diced tomatoes
- 5mL turmeric
- 5mL garam masala
- 2.5mL ground coriander
- 15mL oil
- Salt to taste
- 60mL water
- 160mL cooked rice (dry weight ~55g)

**Nutrition Facts**
- Calories: 500
- Protein: 25g
- Fat: 16g
- Carbs: 65g
- Fiber: 12g

**Pressure Cooker Instructions**
1. Set pressure cooker to sauté mode. Heat 15mL oil.
2. Add diced onion, cook 3-4 min until softened.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add canned tomatoes (with juice), turmeric, garam masala, coriander, and salt. Stir.
5. Add drained chickpeas and 60mL water. Stir.
6. Seal cooker, cook on high pressure for 5 min.
7. Allow natural release for 5 min, then quick release remaining pressure.
8. Stir and adjust seasoning. Serve over rice.

**Stovetop Instructions**
1. Heat 15mL oil in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add diced onion, cook 3-4 min until softened.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook 30 seconds.
4. Add canned tomatoes (with juice), turmeric, garam masala, coriander, and salt. Stir.
5. Add drained chickpeas and 60mL water.
6. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low.
7. Simmer uncovered for 15-20 min until thickened, stirring occasionally.
8. Adjust seasoning and serve over rice.

### Red Lentil Dal (Lunch)
**Prep time:** 20 min | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients**
- 175mL dry red lentils, rinsed
- 355mL water or broth
- 1/2 medium onion, diced (approx 60g)
- 5mL ginger-garlic paste
- 2.5mL turmeric
- 2.5mL garam masala
- 1.25mL ground coriander
- 15mL oil
- Salt to taste
- 160mL cooked rice (dry weight ~55g)

**Nutrition Facts**
- Calories: 480
- Protein: 24g
- Fat: 10g
- Carbs: 70g
- Fiber: 10g

**Pressure Cooker Instructions**
1. Set pressure cooker to sauté mode. Heat 15mL oil.
2. Add diced onion, cook 3-4 min until softened.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, garam masala, and coriander. Cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add rinsed lentils and 355mL water or broth. Stir.
5. Seal cooker, cook on high pressure for 5 min.
6. Allow natural release for 10 min, then quick release any remaining pressure.
7. Stir, season with salt to taste, and serve over rice.

**Stovetop Instructions**
1. Heat 15mL oil in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add diced onion, cook 3-4 min until softened.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, garam masala, and coriander. Cook 30 seconds.
4. Add rinsed lentils and 355mL water or broth.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for 15-20 min until lentils are soft and soup thickens, stirring occasionally.
6. Season with salt to taste and serve over rice.

### Turkey Meatballs + Marinara (Dinner)
**Prep time:** 15 min | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients**
- 150g ground turkey
- Salt, pepper to taste
- 2.5mL dried oregano
- 240mL marinara sauce
- 56g Barilla Protein+ pasta (dry weight) OR 200g spiralized zucchini

**Nutrition Facts**
- Calories: 500
- Protein: 40g
- Fat: 8g
- Carbs: 55g
- Fiber: 4g

**Instructions**
1. Mix ground turkey with salt, pepper, and dried oregano in a bowl.
2. Form mixture into 4-5 meatballs (approximately 30-35g each).
3. Air fry meatballs at 193°C for 10-12 min, turning halfway through, until internal temp reaches 74°C.
4. While meatballs cook, warm marinara sauce in a small pot over medium heat (about 5 min).
5. If using pasta: cook per package directions (typically 8-10 min), drain. If using zucchini noodles: sauté in a nonstick pan for 2-3 min.
6. Combine cooked meatballs with marinara sauce.
7. Serve over cooked pasta or zucchini noodles.

### Black Bean Tacos (Dinner)
**Prep time:** 10 min | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients**
- 1 can (425g) black beans, drained and rinsed (or 310mL cooked beans)
- 2.5mL chili powder
- 2.5mL paprika
- Pinch salt
- 3 corn tortillas
- 1/4 medium avocado, sliced (approx 50g)
- 30mL salsa
- 30mL lactose-free sour cream

**Nutrition Facts**
- Calories: 480
- Protein: 20g
- Fat: 14g
- Carbs: 65g
- Fiber: 12g

## Sunday

### White Bean & Veggie Soup (Dinner)
**Prep time:** 20 min | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients**
- 1 can (425g) white beans, drained and rinsed (or 350mL cooked beans)
- 500mL chopped kale (approx 140g)
- 1 can (400mL) diced tomatoes
- 1/2 medium onion, diced (approx 60g)
- 5mL minced garlic (or 2.5mL garlic paste)
- 480mL broth or water
- 15mL olive oil
- Salt, pepper, dried oregano to taste
- 1 slice (35g) whole grain bread

**Nutrition Facts**
- Calories: 460
- Protein: 23g
- Fat: 12g
- Carbs: 60g
- Fiber: 14g

**Pressure Cooker Instructions**
1. Set pressure cooker to sauté mode. Heat 15mL oil.
2. Add diced onion and garlic, cook 3 min until softened.
3. Add canned tomatoes (with juice), white beans, chopped kale, 480mL broth, salt, pepper, and dried oregano. Stir.
4. Seal cooker, cook on high pressure for 8-10 min.
5. Quick release pressure.
6. Stir and adjust seasoning if needed.
7. Serve with whole grain bread on the side.

**Stovetop Instructions**
1. Heat 15mL oil in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add diced onion and garlic, cook 3 min until softened.
3. Add canned tomatoes (with juice), white beans, chopped kale, 480mL broth, salt, pepper, and dried oregano. Stir.
4. Bring to a simmer, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 15-20 min, stirring occasionally.
5. Adjust seasoning and serve with whole grain bread.

## Morning Wellness Shot (Optional — Make Once Per Week)
**Prep time:** 5 min | **Makes:** 5-6 one-ounce (30mL) shots | **Keeps:** 4-5 days refrigerated

**Ingredients**
- 5cm piece fresh ginger, peeled (approx 15g) OR 30mL ginger paste
- 5mL ground turmeric (or 5cm piece fresh turmeric root)
- 30mL fresh lemon juice (from 2 lemons)
- 1.25mL black pepper
- 45mL raw, unfiltered apple cider vinegar with "the mother"
- 80mL water
- Optional: 5mL honey

**Nutrition Facts (per 1oz/30mL shot)**
- Calories: 10-15
- Protein: 0g
- Fat: 0g
- Carbs: 2-3g
- Fiber: 0g

**Instructions**
1. If using fresh ginger or turmeric root: peel and roughly chop.
2. Add ginger, turmeric, lemon juice, black pepper, apple cider vinegar, water, and honey (if using) to a blender.
3. Blend on high speed until completely smooth (30-60 seconds).
4. If using fresh ginger/turmeric, strain through a fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth to remove fiber.
5. Pour into small glass jars or shot glasses.
6. Refrigerate immediately.
7. Take one 1oz (30mL) shot each morning. Shake before pouring if separation occurs.

**Note:** The live cultures in ACV with the mother provide mild probiotic benefits, but this is not a replacement for your regular probiotic gummy supplement.
