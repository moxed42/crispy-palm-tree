# Sleep, Hydration & Supplements

## Sleep
Given your work hours swing (7am-10pm on intense days), a fixed bedtime isn't realistic — so anchor on **wake time** instead, which matters more for your body's rhythm anyway.

- **Wake time: 6:30am, every day, including weekends.** This is the one non-negotiable. A consistent wake time regulates hunger hormones, energy, and workout performance far more than a consistent bedtime does.
- **Target 7-8 hours of sleep**, meaning bedtime by 10:30-11:30pm depending on when you woke up feeling rested.
- **Wind-down routine (last 30 min before bed):** no food, dim lights, phone away from bed (or on do-not-disturb). If you train in the evening, leave at least 1-2 hours between finishing your workout and getting in bed — training too close to sleep can delay falling asleep.
- **On late work nights (until 10pm):** still hold the 6:30am wake time if possible. One short night is fine; the consistency matters more long-term than any single night's total hours.
- **Use your Fitbit Inspire 3's sleep tracking** to see actual sleep duration and quality, not just time in bed — it'll tell you if you're getting real 7-8 hours or just lying there.

## Hydration
- **Target: half your bodyweight in ounces, minimum** — at ~150 lb, that's ~75 oz (about 9 cups) per day, more on training days or hot days.
- Simplest approach: keep a marked water bottle at your desk (WFH makes this easy) and refill it 2-3x through the day rather than tracking ounces manually.
- Drink a glass of water first thing when you wake up and one with each meal — removes most of the "remembering to drink water" problem on its own.

## Vitamins/supplements
Based on what you actually have:

| Time | Take | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| With breakfast (or mid-morning snack) | Multi + Omega-3 (Women's), B-12 | Take with food for better absorption |
| With lunch | Calcium + D3 gummies | Separate from iron by a few hours (calcium blocks iron absorption) |
| With dinner or evening | Probiotic | Consistency matters more than exact timing — same time daily is the main thing |
| Only on days you haven't had much red meat | Iron | Take with a vitaminC-containing food (citrus, berries) for better absorption, and **away from the calcium gummies and any Metamucil** by a few hours |

- **Omega-3 dose check:** the Nature Made Multi+Omega-3 gummies typically carry a lower EPA/DHA dose than a standalone fish oil softgel — fine as your baseline, but not a substitute if you ever want a therapeutic joint-support dose. Check the actual mg on your label.
- **Metamucil (plain, not orange):** ~25-30g/day fiber target overall — your meals already contribute a good base (chickpeas, black beans, red lentils, vegetables), Metamucil tops it up. Take it **1-2 hours apart from any medication or the iron**, since fiber reduces absorption of both.
- Skip anything else (fat burners, BCAAs, testosterone boosters, etc.) — not shown to meaningfully help recomp.

**One thing worth doing once, not repeating weekly:** if you haven't had bloodwork (vitamin D, B12, iron, thyroid) in the last year, a basic panel would tell you more about actual dosing needs than guessing. Given you're on a high-dose prescription, it's also worth a quick check with your prescriber or pharmacist that the iron/Metamucil timing above doesn't conflict with it — I can give general supplement guidance, but I can't verify interactions with your specific medication and dose.
