## Recipes

### Tofu & Chickpea Bowl (Mon lunch)
**Ingredients**
- 200g extra-firm tofu, pressed, cubed
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 cup canned chickpeas, drained and patted dry
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Pinch salt, paprika (optional, for chickpeas)
- 200g roasted sweet potato (from your batch)
- 1 cup roasted veggies (broccoli/peppers)
- 1-1.5 tbsp tahini
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1-2 tbsp water
- Pinch salt (for dressing)

**Steps**
1. Toss tofu cubes with soy sauce, then cornstarch until coated.
2. Toss chickpeas with olive oil, salt, and paprika.
3. Air fry tofu at 400°F for 12 min, shaking halfway. Air fry chickpeas at 400°F for 10-12 min, shaking halfway (separate batch or a second basket if you have one).
4. Whisk tahini, lemon juice, water, and a pinch of salt until smooth — add more water if too thick.
5. Warm the sweet potato and roasted veggies (microwave 1-2 min, or air fryer 3-4 min).
6. Assemble: sweet potato and veggies as the base, top with tofu and chickpeas, drizzle with tahini sauce.

### Tofu Steaks (Mon dinner)
**Ingredients**
- 200g extra-firm tofu, pressed, sliced into 3-4 slabs
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 cups broccoli and carrots, chopped
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper
- 2/3 cup cooked farro

**Steps**
1. Marinate tofu slices in soy sauce and ginger-garlic paste for at least 10 minutes.
2. Air fry tofu at 400°F for 15 min, flipping halfway.
3. Toss broccoli and carrots with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Air fry at 400°F for 12-15 min (run this batch before or after the tofu if your basket is small).
4. Cook farro ahead of time per package directions, or in the pressure cooker: 1 part farro to 2 parts water, high pressure 10 min, quick release.
5. Plate tofu and veggies over the farro.

### Ground Turkey Taco Bowl (Tue lunch)
**Ingredients**
- 150g ground turkey
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- Pinch salt
- 2/3 cup cooked rice
- 1/2 cup black beans
- Handful shredded lettuce
- 2 tbsp salsa
- 1/4 avocado, sliced

**Steps**
1. Heat a nonstick pan over medium-high heat. Add turkey, breaking it up with a spatula as it cooks.
2. Season with chili powder, paprika, oregano, and salt. Cook 8-10 min until browned through (internal temp 165°F).
3. Warm the black beans separately (microwave or small pot, 2-3 min).
4. Build the bowl: rice on the bottom, turkey and beans on top, then lettuce, salsa, and avocado.

### Ground Beef & Veggie Stir-Fry (Tue dinner)
**Ingredients**
- 150g Walden Local 80/20 ground beef
- 2 cups mixed stir-fry veggies (fresh or frozen)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil (optional)
- 2/3 cup cooked rice

**Steps**
1. Brown the beef in a pan over medium-high heat, breaking it apart, about 6-8 min.
2. Drain the fat (tilt the pan and spoon it off, or transfer to a strainer and rinse briefly with hot water for a leaner result).
3. Add the ginger-garlic paste, cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add the veggies and soy sauce, stir-fry 4-5 min until crisp-tender.
5. Serve over rice.

### Chickpea Salad (Wed lunch)
**Ingredients**
- 2 cups mixed greens
- 1.5 cups chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cucumber, sliced
- 2 tbsp lactose-free feta or a handful of olives
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp vinegar (red wine or apple cider)
- Salt, pepper

**Steps**
1. Combine greens, chickpeas, cucumber, and feta or olives in a bowl.
2. Whisk olive oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper. Drizzle over the salad and toss.

### Chana Masala (Wed dinner)
**Ingredients**
- 1.75 cups canned chickpeas, drained
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1-2 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 cup canned diced tomatoes
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tbsp oil
- Salt to taste
- 2/3 cup cooked rice

**Steps (pressure cooker)**
1. Sauté mode: heat oil, add onion, cook 3-4 min until soft.
2. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook 30 sec.
3. Add tomatoes, turmeric, garam masala, coriander, and salt. Stir.
4. Add chickpeas and 1/4 cup water. Seal, cook on high pressure 5 min, natural release 5 min, then quick release.
5. Serve over rice.

**Stovetop version:** same steps, then simmer uncovered 15-20 min after adding the chickpeas, until thickened.

### Red Lentil Dal (Thu lunch)
**Ingredients**
- 3/4 cup dry red lentils, rinsed
- 1.5 cups water or broth
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- 1/4 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tbsp oil
- Salt to taste
- 2/3 cup cooked rice

**Steps (pressure cooker)**
1. Sauté mode: cook onion in oil, 3-4 min.
2. Add ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, garam masala, and coriander, cook 30 sec.
3. Add rinsed lentils and water/broth. Seal, cook on high pressure 5 min, natural release 10 min.
4. Stir, season with salt, serve over rice.

### Turkey Meatballs + Marinara (Thu dinner)
**Ingredients**
- 150g ground turkey
- Salt, pepper, 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 cup marinara sauce
- 2oz Barilla Protein+ pasta (or zucchini noodles)

**Steps**
1. Mix ground turkey with oregano, salt, and pepper. Form into 4-5 meatballs.
2. Air fry at 380°F for 10-12 min, turning halfway, until internal temp reaches 165°F.
3. Warm marinara in a small pot while the meatballs cook.
4. Cook pasta per package directions, or sauté spiralized zucchini 2-3 min.
5. Combine meatballs with marinara, serve over pasta or zoodles.

### Meatball Bowl (Fri lunch)
Same meatball recipe as Thursday — make a double batch on Thursday if you want to cover both days at once. Serve with roasted veggies and 2/3 cup farro or rice instead of marinara and pasta.

### Black Bean Tacos (Fri dinner)
**Ingredients**
- 1.25 cups canned black beans, drained
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- Pinch salt
- 3 corn tortillas
- 1/4 avocado, sliced
- 2 tbsp salsa
- Lactose-free sour cream

**Steps**
1. Warm the black beans in a small pot with chili powder, paprika, and salt, mashing lightly with a fork if you want a thicker texture, about 5 min.
2. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan (30 sec per side) or microwave wrapped in a damp paper towel (30 sec).
3. Fill tortillas with beans, top with avocado, salsa, and sour cream.

### White Bean & Veggie Soup (Sun dinner)
**Ingredients**
- 1.75 cups canned white beans, drained
- 2 cups chopped kale
- 1 cup canned diced tomatoes
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 2 cups broth or water
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper, dried oregano to taste
- 1 slice whole grain bread

**Steps (pressure cooker)**
1. Sauté mode: heat olive oil, add onion and garlic, cook 3 min.
2. Add tomatoes, beans, kale, broth, salt, pepper, and oregano.
3. Seal, cook on high pressure 8-10 min, then release.
4. Serve with the bread.

## Quick fallback options (for when there's no time to cook)
Grab-and-eat, no real prep, ~350-450 kcal / 20-30g protein with the pairing:

**Fallback A — Tuna/chicken pouch + veggies**
2 Starkist tuna pouches (2.5-2.6oz each) + baby carrots + a piece of fruit. ~300 kcal / 26g protein. Chicken breast pouches work the same way if you want to switch it up.

**Fallback B — Hard-boiled egg + hummus**
2 pre-made hard-boiled eggs + an individual hummus cup + baby carrots or cucumber. ~330 kcal / 18g protein. *(Pair with a Slate shake if you want it closer to 25-30g.)*

**Fallback C — Frozen meatballs, microwaved**
Keep a bag of fully-cooked frozen turkey (or plant-based) meatballs in the freezer. 4-5 meatballs, microwave ~90 sec, eat with a side salad or fruit. ~320 kcal / 24g protein.

**Fallback D — Uncrustable, upgraded**
Pair it with a Slate shake or 2 hard-boiled eggs on the side — brings it to ~380-420 kcal, 27-32g protein.

**Fallback E — Frozen protein bowl**
Keep 1-2 in the freezer as a last resort. Look for at least 20g protein per serving and no dairy on the label — check each one at the store since formulations vary.

**Simple adjustment rule:** if whatever you grab is under ~20g protein, add a hard-boiled egg or half a Slate shake alongside it.

## Morning wellness shot (optional add-on)
Makes ~5-6 one-ounce shots, keeps refrigerated 4-5 days.

**Ingredients**
- 2-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled (or 2 tbsp ginger paste)
- 1 tsp ground turmeric (or 2-inch piece fresh turmeric root if you can find it)
- Juice of 3 lemons
- 1/4 tsp black pepper — not optional, this increases turmeric's active compound (curcumin) absorption dramatically
- 2 tbsp raw, unfiltered apple cider vinegar with "the mother"
- 1/2 cup water
- Optional: 1 tsp honey to cut the sharpness

**Steps**
1. Blend everything until smooth.
2. If using fresh ginger/turmeric root, strain through a fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth to remove fiber.
3. Pour into small jars or a shot-glass-sized container, refrigerate, take one shot (1oz) each morning.

**Note on the "probiotic" part:** the ACV with the mother is what carries any live culture here, and it's a mild contribution — nowhere near as reliable as your actual probiotic gummy.

## Brand/label guide for the processed items
- **Granola:** target ~140-160 kcal per 1/4 cup serving, 4g+ protein, under 6g added sugar. Bear Naked Protein granola runs ~11g protein per 59g (~1/2 cup) serving at 260 kcal.
- **Marinara sauce:** under 90 kcal and under 6g sugar per 1/2 cup. Rao's and similar "no sugar added" lines are a safe bet.
- **Salsa:** almost all low-calorie regardless of brand (~10-15 kcal per 2 tbsp) — pick what tastes good.
- **Corn tortillas:** fairly consistent across brands, ~50-60 kcal each.
- **Canned beans/chickpeas:** go for "no salt added" or "low sodium" versions where available.
- **Frozen protein bowls:** target 20g+ protein per bowl, under 500 kcal, no dairy on the ingredient list.
- **Lactose-free yogurt:** Green Valley Creamery and Lactaid are the two most widely available options.
- **Tahini:** Soom comes out on top across multiple independent taste tests. Sesame King is a solid standard-supermarket alternative.

## A note on precision
Since you have a food scale, use it for the protein sources especially (tofu, ground meat) and the starches — weighing gets you much closer to the calorie/protein numbers in this plan than eyeballing cups does. Rough guide: 2/3 cup cooked rice ≈ 130g, 200g roasted sweet potato ≈ similar calories to 2/3 cup rice, 150g ground meat ≈ raw weight before cooking.
