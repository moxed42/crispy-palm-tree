# Week 1 — Meal Plan (Recomp)
**Target: ~1,550-1,650 kcal/day, ~120-135g protein.** Estimates from standard nutrition data, not lab-measured — log it for 2 weeks in an app (Cronometer is good for protein accuracy) if you want to fine-tune, and I'll adjust from real results.

---

## Daily template (repeat all 7 days)

**Early morning — Coffee + light protein** (~100-120 kcal, 8-10g protein)
- Coffee as you take it (splash of oat milk)
- 1 hard-boiled egg, OR a half-scoop protein shake — whichever you actually want when you're not hungry yet

**Mid-morning — Protein snack** (~10:30-11am, ~280-320 kcal, 24-26g protein)
- 3/4 cup lactose-free plain Greek-style yogurt + 1/4 cup granola + handful berries + 1 hard-boiled egg on the side

**Afternoon snack — Protein shake** (~160 kcal, ~30g protein)
- 1 Slate Ultra Protein shake — check your can's label since exact numbers vary slightly by flavor

## Quick fallback options (for when there's no time to cook)
Grab-and-eat, no real prep, ~350-450 kcal / 20-30g protein with the pairing:

**Fallback A — Chicken pouch + veggies**
2 Starkist chicken breast pouches (2.5oz each) + baby carrots + a piece of fruit. ~300 kcal / 26g protein.

**Fallback B — Hard-boiled egg + hummus**
Batch-boil 6-8 eggs once at the start of the week and keep them in the fridge. Grab 2 + an individual hummus cup + baby carrots or cucumber. ~330 kcal / 18g protein. *(Pair with a Slate shake if you want it closer to 25-30g.)*

**Fallback C — Frozen meatballs, microwaved**
Keep a bag of fully-cooked frozen turkey (or plant-based) meatballs in the freezer. 4-5 meatballs, microwave ~90 sec, eat with a side salad or fruit. ~320 kcal / 24g protein.

**Fallback D — Uncrustable, upgraded**
Pair it with a Slate shake or 2 hard-boiled eggs on the side — brings it to ~380-420 kcal, 27-32g protein.

**Fallback E — Frozen protein bowl**
Keep 1-2 in the freezer as a last resort. Look for at least 20g protein per serving and no dairy on the label — check each one at the store since formulations vary.

**Simple adjustment rule:** if whatever you grab is under ~20g protein, add a hard-boiled egg or half a Slate shake alongside it.

## Brand/label guide for the processed items

- **Granola:** target ~140-160 kcal per 1/4 cup serving, 4g+ protein, under 6g added sugar. Bear Naked Protein granola runs ~11g protein per 59g (~1/2 cup) serving at 260 kcal — good protein-to-calorie ratio.
- **Marinara sauce:** under 90 kcal and under 6g sugar per 1/2 cup. Rao's and similar "no sugar added" lines are a safe bet.
- **Salsa:** almost all low-calorie regardless of brand (~10-15 kcal per 2 tbsp) — pick what tastes good.
- **Corn tortillas:** fairly consistent across brands, ~50-60 kcal each.
- **Canned beans/chickpeas:** go for "no salt added" or "low sodium" versions where available.
- **Frozen protein bowls:** target 20g+ protein per bowl, under 500 kcal, no dairy on the ingredient list — check each one at the store.
- **Lactose-free yogurt:** Green Valley Creamery and Lactaid are the two most widely available options.
- **Tahini:** Soom comes out on top across multiple independent taste tests (smooth, balanced, not bitter) and is widely available at Whole Foods and online. If it's not at your store, Sesame King is a solid, more standard-supermarket alternative.

## A note on precision
Since you have a food scale, use it for the protein sources especially (tofu, ground meat) and the starches — weighing gets you much closer to the calorie/protein numbers in this plan than eyeballing cups does. Rough guide: 2/3 cup cooked rice ≈ 130g, 200g roasted sweet potato ≈ similar calories to 2/3 cup rice, 150g ground meat ≈ raw weight before cooking.

Since you're batch-cooking sweet potatoes today, that covers Monday's lunch and any other meal where you want to swap it in for rice/farro through the week.
