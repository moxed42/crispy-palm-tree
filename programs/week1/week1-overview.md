# Week 1 — Recomp Plan Overview
Starting Tuesday. This is the index for the full plan — four documents, each covering one piece.

## The four documents
1. **week1-strength-plan.md** — 4-day upper/lower split, equipment-specific (squat wedges, plywood platform, EZ curl bar, TRX, etc.), hypermobility-adjusted cues, daily step target
2. **week1-meal-plan.md** — daily meal template, lunch/dinner rotation, fallback options, full grocery list grouped by aisle, brand/label guidance
3. **sleep-hydration-supplements.md** — wake time anchor, hydration target, your actual supplement stack with a timing schedule
4. **This document** — ties them together, holds the numbers that span more than one area

## The core numbers
- **Calories:** ~1,550-1,650 kcal/day
- **Protein:** ~120-135g/day
- **Steps:** 8,000-10,000/day to start (working toward 10-20k)
- **Training:** 4 days/week, upper/lower split
- **Sleep:** wake time anchored at 6:30am, 7-8 hours target
- **Water:** ~75oz/day minimum

## Known context this plan is built around
- Home gym (well-equipped — see strength plan for full equipment list), low-pile basement carpet, ~75-80in ceiling
- Hypermobility (self-managed, no PT guidance) — informs training cues throughout
- Desk job, WFH, no driving, groceries via delivery
- Lactose intolerant, plant-leaning but not vegetarian, occasional meat-averse days
- On a high-dose prescription — relevant to supplement timing (see supplements doc)
- Fitbit Inspire 3 for step/sleep tracking

## Things intentionally not started yet
- **Running 1 consecutive mile** and **jump rope boxer step** — revisit in ~3-4 weeks once the current routine feels automatic
- Spot fat loss around midsection/hips isn't a separate action — it's the natural (if slower) result of the existing plan, not something to add to

## If something isn't working
- **Hunger/energy crashing:** check total calories are actually landing at 1,550-1,650, not drifting lower
- **A specific exercise isn't "feeling" right (glute bridge, hip thrust):** try the cueing tweaks in the strength doc; if it still doesn't land, drop it — not everyone needs every exercise
- **Kneecap or trap/neck issues recurring:** worth an actual PT visit, not just chat-based workarounds
- **Scale not moving after 2-3 weeks of accurate tracking:** that's the point to reassess calories, not before
