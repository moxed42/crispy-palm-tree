# First Chin-Up Plan

Train Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 2026-10-05 for 8 weeks, rest two
days, then test the first chin-up on 2026-12-02. Three phases take you from
a thick assist band to an unassisted chin-up, with the band getting lighter
and the negatives slower each phase:

- **Phase 1, Foundation (weeks 1-3, Oct 5 - Oct 25):** thickest band, 4 sec
  negatives. Focus: clean reps, shoulder control.
- **Phase 2, Strength (weeks 4-6, Oct 26 - Nov 15):** thinner band, 5 sec
  negatives. Focus: feet further forward on TRX rows.
- **Phase 3, Convert (weeks 7-8, Nov 16 - Nov 29):** thinnest band, 5 sec
  negatives. Focus: one unassisted attempt at the start of Day 1 each week.
- **Rest Nov 30 - Dec 1, Test Dec 2.**

Every session: 5-minute warm-up of band pull-aparts 2x12 and scap pulls 2x6.
Rest 2 minutes between pulling sets. Stop every set 1-2 reps short of
failure. Hollow hold scaling: knees tucked (10-15 sec) -> legs high at ~45
degrees -> legs low with arms overhead; move up only when your lower back
stays flat on the floor the whole hold.

**Progression:** within a phase, hit the top of the rep range on every set
of an exercise -> make it harder (thinner band, slower tempo, feet further
forward on TRX rows). Stuck — can't reach 4x4 band-assisted chin-ups with
the thinner band by end of week 6? Repeat phase 2 another week rather than
moving on.

## Day 1 (2026-10-05 Mon) — Negatives
| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Weight | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-Up | 2x12 + 2x6 | band pull-aparts, then scap pulls — ~5 min, get the shoulders moving before pulling | Band |
| Chin-Up Negatives | 4x3 | step up to chin over bar, lower for 4 sec down | Pull-up Bar |
| TRX Rows | 3x8-10 | walk feet forward once 10 reps clean | TRX |
| Scap Pulls, palms away | 3x6-8 | arms straight, shoulders pull down, elbows soft | Pull-up Bar |
| Hollow Hold | 3x20 sec | scale: knees tucked -> legs high ~45° -> legs low overhead | Bodyweight |
| Cool-Down | 2-3 min | shake out arms, shoulder rolls, easy walk — no static stretching (works against joint stability with hypermobility) | Bodyweight |

## Day 25 (2026-12-02 Wed) — Chin-Up Test
| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Weight | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-Up | 2x12 + 2x6 | band pull-aparts, then scap pulls — same as every session | Band |
| Chin-Up Test Attempt | up to 3x1 | active hang start, no swing/kick, chin over bar, controlled lower; 2-3 min between attempts | Pull-up Bar |
| Cool-Down | 2-3 min | shake out arms, shoulder rolls, easy walk — no static stretching (works against joint stability with hypermobility) | Bodyweight |

A rep counts if you start from an active hang, pull without swinging or
kicking, get your chin over the bar, and lower under control. A first
chin-up in 60 days is plausible but not guaranteed — if close but missed,
repeat phase 3 and test again two weeks later.
