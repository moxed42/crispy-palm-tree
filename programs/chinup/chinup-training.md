# First Chin-Up Plan

Train Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 2026-10-05 for 12 weeks, rest four
days, then test the first chin-up on 2027-01-01. Four phases take you from
a thick assist band to an unassisted chin-up, with the band getting lighter
and the negatives slower each phase:

- **Phase 1, Foundation (weeks 1-3, Oct 5 - Oct 25):** thickest band, 2-3
  sec negatives. Focus: clean reps, shoulder control.
- **Phase 2, Build (weeks 4-6, Oct 26 - Nov 15):** thick band, 4 sec
  negatives. Focus: add reps before changing bands.
- **Phase 3, Strength (weeks 7-9, Nov 16 - Dec 6):** thinner band, 5 sec
  negatives. Focus: feet further forward on TRX rows.
- **Phase 4, Convert (weeks 10-12, Dec 7 - Dec 27):** thinnest band, 5 sec
  negatives. Focus: one unassisted attempt at the start of Day 1 each week.
- **Rest Dec 28 - Dec 31, Test Jan 1.**

Every session: 5-minute warm-up of band pull-aparts 2x8 with your lightest
band and scap pulls 2x3 (stop short of tired — the warm-up should not use up
your shoulders before the main work). Rest 2 minutes between pulling sets.
Stop every set 1-2 reps short of failure. Hollow hold scaling: knees tucked
(10-15 sec) -> legs high at ~45 degrees -> legs low with arms overhead; move
up only when your lower back stays flat on the floor the whole hold.

**Week 1-2 adjustment:** Day 1 showed the starting level was lower than
planned, so weeks 1-2 use easier versions of two Day 1 exercises (the
weekly schedule itself doesn't change) — see those two sessions below for
the substituted rows. Reassess at the end of week 4: if band-assisted
chin-ups aren't yet 3x4 with the thickest band, the Jan 1 test gets harder
to hit and the date gets reassessed.

**Progression:** within a phase, hit the top of the rep range on every set
of an exercise -> make it harder (thinner band, slower tempo, feet further
forward on TRX rows). Stuck — can't reach 4x4 band-assisted chin-ups with
the thinner band by end of week 9? Repeat phase 3 another week rather than
moving on.

Because pulls mostly get felt in the arms rather than the back, every
session now includes a light Straight-Arm Pulldown to practice engaging
the lats specifically — same cue on every pulling exercise: shoulders down
first, then elbows toward the hip pockets. This isn't backed by trial
evidence that it speeds up a first chin-up — it's motor practice, so keep
it light.

## Day 1 (2026-10-05 Mon) — Negatives
| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Weight | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-Up | 2x8 + 2x3 | band pull-aparts (lightest band), then scap pulls — stop short of tired, don't use up the shoulders before the main work | Band |
| Chin-Up Negatives (band-assisted, weeks 1-2) | 3x3 | thickest band looped over bar, knee or foot in it, lower over 3 sec — move to unassisted negatives once this feels controlled for 2 weeks straight | Band/Pull-up Bar |
| TRX Rows | 3x8-10 | walk feet forward once 10 reps clean | TRX |
| Scap Pulls, palms away (weeks 1-2) | 3x3 | 60-90 sec rest counts as the work — add 1 rep/set once all sets feel clean, until 3x6 | Pull-up Bar |
| Hollow Hold | 3x20 sec | scale: knees tucked -> legs high ~45° -> legs low overhead | Bodyweight |
| Straight-Arm Pulldown (pulley or band) | 2x8-10 | light; arms stay straight, head height to thighs — if you feel it in your arms, use less weight | Cable/Band |
| Cool-Down | 2-3 min | shake out arms, shoulder rolls, easy walk — no static stretching (works against joint stability with hypermobility) | Bodyweight |
