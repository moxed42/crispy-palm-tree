# Guidelines

## Week 1 Ramp-In
Going straight into full programmed volume in week 1 produces the worst soreness of the whole block for no long-term benefit — a lighter first week actually reduces soreness in the weeks that follow (the "repeated bout effect"). Soreness isn't a quality signal either: a session can build strength with little to no DOMS, so don't add volume in Week 2 just because Week 1 wasn't sore. Mild, symmetric soreness that peaks 24-72 hrs and fades over 5-7 days is normal DOMS; sharp pain, pain isolated to one joint, swelling, or pain at rest is not — treat that differently.
- Sets: 2 instead of 3 on every exercise programmed for 3. Exercises already at 2 sets can stay at 2 (or drop to 1 if you want to be extra conservative).
- Effort: RIR 4-5 instead of RIR 1-3 — stop noticeably short of failure; the last rep of every set should feel clearly manageable.
- Load: no change — the weights in the workout tables are already discounted for the layoff.
- From Week 2 on: resume the full programmed sets and RIR 1-3 as written.

## Warm-Up (every session, ~8-12 min)
Skip static stretching entirely — with hypermobility, it works against joint stability rather than for it.
- 4-5 min light cardio — bike, brisk walk, or jumping jacks. Get breathing up slightly, not winded.
- Dynamic mobility (Upper A & B), no static holds: arm circles x10/direction each arm, band pull-aparts x15, scap push-ups x10 (shoulder blade protraction/retraction, elbows soft), wrist circles x10/direction each hand (given the wrist history — do this even on days wrist roller isn't programmed).
- Dynamic mobility (Lower A & B), no static holds: leg swings x10/side (front-back, then side-side), bodyweight squats x10, hip circles x10/side, ankle rocks x10/side.
- Ramp-up, Upper A: DB Bench Press — 1x10 empty hands/very light DB, 1x5 at ~50% working weight. DB Shoulder Press — 1x8 at a light weight (5-8 lb) before working sets, given the shoulder history.
- Ramp-up, Upper B: DB Floor Press or Incline Push-ups — 1x8-10 at reduced range/incline before working sets. DB Shoulder Press — same as Upper A, 1x8 light before working sets.
- Ramp-up, Lower A & B: Barbell Box Squat — 1x10 bodyweight box squat, 1x5 at ~50% working weight. RDL — 1x8 with an empty bar or very light DB before working sets — a separate movement pattern (hip hinge, not knee-dominant) from the squat, so it gets its own ramp-up rather than riding the squat's warm-up.
- Isolation/accessory work (rows, curls, face pulls, calf raises, tib raises) skips ramp-up sets entirely — just start at the prescribed working weight.

## Rules for Every Session
- Weekly order: Upper A, then Lower A, then Upper B, then Lower B, with a rest day between where possible (e.g. Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri). Avoid two lower days back to back with no rest.
- No locked-out joints at the top of any lift — soft elbow on presses, soft knee on squats/split squats. This is the hypermobility cue — non-negotiable.
- Effort target: RIR 1-3 (stop 1-3 reps short of failure) on every working set.
- Progression: once every set of an exercise hits the top of its rep range cleanly, add the smallest available jump — 2.5-5 lb total on barbell lifts using fractional plates, one more rep or the next DB increment on dumbbell work.
- Rest: 90 sec between sets on isolation work (curls, tricep extensions, face pulls, calves), 2 min on compounds (squat, bench, row, RDL, OHP variations).

## Time Per Session
- Upper days (A and B): ~60-70 minutes including warm-up.
- Lower days (A and B): ~50-65 minutes including warm-up.
- If running long, cut rest on isolation work to 60-75 seconds first — never cut it from the compounds.
